# ATS Scoring & Career Intelligence Agent Rules

When modifying ATS scoring math, diagnostic feedback, or bullet improvements:

1. **6-Dimension Scoring Balance:**
   - ATS Parseability: 20%
   - Content Quality: 20%
   - Skill Density: 20%
   - Experience Impact: 20%
   - Layout & Formatting: 10%
   - Projects & Portfolio: 10%
   - The sum of weights must strictly equal 100%.

2. **Google X-Y-Z Formula Enforcement:**
   - Every bullet rewrite must follow: *"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"*.
   - Never generate vague advice without quantified examples.
